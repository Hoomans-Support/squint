# Product

**Name:** Squint. Mark: a blue rounded square with a white S (`app/static/logo.svg`).

**What it is:** a diagnostic dashboard for product managers. It reads raw app and web event logs, finds the places where users struggle (including small groups that overall numbers hide), and explains each one in plain English with the original records as proof.

**Who uses it:** product managers first. Also UX researchers and designers, data analysts, growth teams. They open it to answer "where are users getting stuck, and why?" without writing queries.

**Mode:** Operate. The visitor is doing a job (finding and judging problems), so the screen is a working product, not a pitch.

**Principles from the PRD**
- Show findings, not charts to interpret. A short ranked list of what needs attention.
- Respect natural exits. Users leaving a pricing page after reading it is not a problem, and the product must say so.
- No-force philosophy. Recommendations never push users with pop-ups or forced steps.
- Every claim traces to raw records. Nothing is shown that the data does not support.

**Honesty rules**
- All demo data is synthetic and the UI says so.
- Dollar figures rest on a stated placeholder assumption and are labelled as such.
- Fields the AI has not filled in yet are shown as "not diagnosed yet", never invented.

**Anti-references:** generic analytics dashboards (a wall of KPI tiles and sparklines), marketing landing pages, chat-first interfaces.
